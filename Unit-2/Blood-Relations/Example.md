# Blood Relations — Examples

## Questions

### Question 1

Introducing a woman, A said, “Her husband is the only son of my father.”

How is the woman related to A?

a) Sister
b) Wife
c) Mother
d) Daughter

### Question 2

A and B are the children of C. If C is the father of A but B is not the son of C, then what is the relationship between B and C?

a) Daughter
b) Son
c) Sister
d) Brother

### Question 3

If A#B means A is the father of B, A*B means A is the brother of B, and A@B means A is the mother of B, then which of the following is correct about G@T#P?

a) G is mother of P
b) P is father of T
c) T is son of G
d) P is brother of T
